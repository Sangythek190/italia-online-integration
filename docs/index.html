💡 Why This Integration Exists

Italia Online needed to migrate their 130,000 SMB billing infrastructure to Stripe Connect (Standard) for automated payment processing. Their 700+ sales agents required a dashboard to manage their SMB portfolios, and product activation needed to happen automatically post-payment instead of through manual provisioning.

I designed the Stripe Connect integration architecture, managed the billing migration with zero-downtime cutover, and planned the Bring Your Own Payment Gateway automation as the next phase. Cross-border coordination between North American engineering and Italian business stakeholders, with GDPR data residency requirements adding complexity at every step.
