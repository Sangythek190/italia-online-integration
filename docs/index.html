💡 Why This Integration

The AI Voice Receptionist answers calls for businesses — but cannot complete a booking because there is no integration with any scheduling system. The partner has 40,000 websites on the booking platform, and this integration enables a new Voice Receptionist + Booking package for all 40,000 businesses — a new revenue stream at 1,399 to 2,500 euros per year per business.

I wrote the mission brief, defined 4 MCP booking tools, managed cross-team delivery, and navigated a mid-build API migration when the booking platform replaced their API with a new AI-agent-specific Proxy API.

Live Demo

Demo Walkthrough

End-to-end demo on a test business running on the booking platform sandbox with the AI Chat Receptionist booking appointments in real time.

1 Connect Booking Platform

Operations team opens business application → Administration → Integrations → Booking Platform. Enters the Site ID, selects Europe/Rome timezone. System validates credentials with a test API call. Status: Connected. Setup time: under 10 minutes.

2 AI Lists Services

Customer asks “what services do you offer?” — the AI calls the booking API and returns all bookable services with duration and pricing: Men’s Haircut ($25, 30 min), Women’s Cut & Blow-Dry ($55, 60 min), Kids’ Haircut (free, 20 min), Fringe/Bang Trim (free, 15 min).

3 AI Checks Availability

Customer asks “what slots are available for kids haircut?” — the AI queries the scheduling API and returns real-time availability: “Today 11:20 AM, 11:40 AM, 12:00 PM… Tomorrow 9:00 AM and throughout the day in 20-minute intervals.”

4 AI Books the Appointment

Customer says “book at 9AM” — the AI collects name and email, then creates the booking in the business calendar. Confirmation: “Your Kids’ Haircut has been booked for Friday, August 14 at 9:00 AM.” Service, date, time, name, and email all confirmed.

5 Full Conversation in Partner Dashboard

The complete booking conversation appears in the partner dashboard — every message, every detail, fully attributed to the Chat Receptionist. The business owner and operations team can see exactly what the AI said, what the customer requested, and that the booking was confirmed. Voice call history visible in the same inbox alongside chat conversations.

Integration Details

Click any card to expand technical and process details for each integration component.

📅 Booking Tools (MCP)

▼
  • List appointment types — what services the business offers
  • Check availability — free time slots for a specific service and date
  • Create appointment — book with customer name, phone, email, notes
  • Look up booking — find existing appointments (removed in new API, replaced with email link)
  • Idempotency keys prevent double-booking on timeout retries
  • Timezone-aware scheduling — business timezone set on connection

🤖 AI Capability Architecture

▼
  • Booking capability card registered in the business application
  • Same pattern as existing reference pattern for AI reservation capability
  • Works with both Voice AI employees (phone calls) and Chat AI employees (web chat)
  • Capability scoped to ASSISTANT_TYPE with access control
  • Secure per-account credential storage — one set per business
  • Setup target: under 10 minutes per business

⚡ Payment Integration

▼
